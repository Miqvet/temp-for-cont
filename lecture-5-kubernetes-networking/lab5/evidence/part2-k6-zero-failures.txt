(see evidence/part2-chaos-log.txt).
Client: k6 load.js, 10 VUs, 90s, hitting http://shop-api (ClusterIP by DNS name,
NOT a pod IP, NOT the NodePort) from inside the cluster.

First attempt (before the preStop fix) showed 5/5686 requests failing (0.08%) —
traced to the api's SIGTERM handling: server.Shutdown() closes the HTTP listener
immediately, but the readiness probe (same /health endpoint) only gets re-checked
every periodSeconds, so kube-proxy/EndpointSlice has a brief window where it still
routes to a pod whose port is already closed. Added a `lifecycle.preStop.exec`
hook (`sleep 5`) to api/worker deployments so the pod keeps serving during the
window the readiness probe needs to notice SIGTERM and pull it from the Service
*before* the listener actually closes. Rerun after the fix: 0/2544 failed.
